How a furnace replacement is planned

The exact process depends on the home and the equipment, so confirm the details directly rather than relying on assumptions. A useful starting checklist includes:

  • Describe the current furnace and any problem that led you to consider replacement.
  • Ask what details about the home and existing system are needed to assess the project.
  • Find out what equipment options are being considered and why.
  • Confirm what the proposed work includes and whether anything is separate from the installation.
  • Ask how the old equipment and the new system will be handled during the project.

Avoid choosing a system based only on a brief description or an unverified assumption about what your home needs. Don’t agree to work until the scope and recommendations make sense to you. If you notice a problem before the appointment, record what happens and share those details rather than trying to open or repair the equipment yourself.

Should I replace my furnace if it stops working?

Not necessarily, since a stopped furnace may need assessment before you decide what to do. Describe the symptoms when you call and ask what kind of service is appropriate.
