What a maintenance visit can cover

The specific work should be confirmed when you book, since equipment and concerns differ from one home to another. Before the appointment, use this short checklist:

  • Note any changes in sound, airflow, or how the system starts and stops.
  • Write down when you first noticed the issue and whether it happens often.
  • Make a list of questions about the system’s operation or suggested follow-up.
  • Ask what access the technician will need and whether you should prepare anything.
  • Request an explanation of findings in plain language before deciding on additional work.

Try not to assume a maintenance visit automatically includes a repair or every possible inspection. Be specific about what you want addressed, and confirm the planned scope before work begins. If the system has stopped working or the issue seems urgent, call and describe the problem rather than waiting for a routine appointment.
